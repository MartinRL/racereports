Race reports from unsupported ultracycling events. English by default; svenska under [🇸🇪 Svenska](sv/).

## Reports

- [Vätternrundan 2019](vatternrundan-2019): 297 km around lake Vättern, sub 7 h with Team Øresund
- [Race Around Denmark 2020](race-around-denmark-2020): 1 567 km / 9 616 m around Denmark, 3rd of 18
- [BRM 1200 Stockholm 2020](brm-1200-2020): 1 210 km / 9 342 m brevet with Randonneur Stockholm
- [Race Around Denmark 2021](race-around-denmark-2021): 1 610 km / 10 312 m around Denmark, 4th
- [Sverigetempot 2021](sverigetempot-2021): Length of Sweden, 2 123 km / 15 870 m, joint 2nd of ~120
- [1001 Miglia 2021](1001-miglia-2021): 1 610 km / 15 500 m through Italy, 20th of 305
- [Race Around Denmark 2022](race-around-denmark-2022): 1 609 km / 11 530 m around Denmark, 2nd
- [Race Around Poland 2022](race-around-poland-2022): DNF after 315 km, rear derailleur failure
- [Race Around Denmark 2023](race-around-denmark-2023): 1 607 km / 10 192 m around Denmark, 4th of 19
- [Race Around Poland 2023](race-around-poland-2023): 3 618 km / 33 000 m around Poland, 11th of 23
- [RatN 2026](ratn-2026): Race around the Netherlands, 1 951 km, 16th of 127
